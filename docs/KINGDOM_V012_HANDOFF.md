2. Preserve existing IDs, links, forms and storage contracts while changing visual wrappers. User's next screenshots are authoritative for page-specific refinements.
3. Live AI bots need a real backend/provider, privacy controls and verified responses. Never relabel prepared dialogue as live AI.
4. Real jobs, social/OAuth, cross-posting, donations, paid time, cooperative membership and ownership need implemented services and explicit terms. Current copy is a proposal/demo; do not invent availability.
5. Photo memorial avatars: later phase with permitted photos, clear memorial/AI labels, family control and deletion.
6. Keep the exact three motto lines. No old Site URLs should be substituted for this app's internal tools.
