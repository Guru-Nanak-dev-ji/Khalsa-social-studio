## Public-first commitments proposed for Sangat consultation
1. Core KDN public experience free to use; no mandatory investment, donation or GitHub/AI account to submit an idea.
2. Panth-first service, transparent community stewardship, no individual claim of absolute ownership. Legal title, trustees and licensing remain undecided.
3. Protected design: royal gold, mehrab, original narrative, Pran Dwar and 3D heritage cannot be changed without documented legitimate community approval.
4. Equal dignity and essential access regardless of money, religious identity or reward points.
5. A public amendment portal with proposals, accessible popups, human moderation, reasoned decisions, change history and anti-abuse safeguards. Browser popups/in-app notices can be built first; OS push requires opt-in, permission and backend.

## Partnership and financing — NOT APPROVED
- Proposal from community discussion: contributors may be partners; an investor spending on the project may seek up to twice the original amount from profits within 0–5 years.
- This is NOT a guaranteed return, legal entitlement or currently available investment product. There may be no profits. Legal classification may involve securities, crowdfunding, consumer, charity/nonprofit and tax rules across countries. No funds or shares should be solicited on this draft.
- Require qualified local legal/tax review, written risk disclosure, audited accounting, treatment of losses, liquidity, cap tables, eligibility, voting rights, conflict-of-interest rules, and separation of donation vs equity vs loan vs reimbursable expense.
- If the organization is charitable/nonprofit, private profit distribution may be prohibited or constrained. Choose legal structure before any share pricing.
- Dasvandh 10% contributions: optional, verifiable, privacy-respecting service recognition points may be proposed, but no purchased voting control, no privileged fundamental rights, and no implication of guaranteed financial return. Consult community and legal advisors about charity/tax treatment.

## 30 social/network accounts — connection policy
Do not list private account credentials publicly. Inventory each platform privately with its owner, API support, permission scopes, rate limits, cost and revocation. Start with ONE authorized low-risk connector and test it. No bulk account linkage, posting or private-data transfer without explicit account-owner consent. Some platforms may not offer suitable APIs.

## Mobile 'one click' experience
A KDN Home screen should expose Ideas, My Tasks, AI Handoff, News, Network status, Constitution, FAQ and Notifications. One tap can open the dashboard, not bypass platform authentication or security. Android app and website share backend, with roles and permissions.

## Governance review questions
- Which legal jurisdiction(s), and which community body can approve governance?
- Who legally holds code, trademarks, domains and assets until stewardship is established?
- Which content/software licenses and contributor agreements?
- How to prevent conflicts between free public service and investor profit distributions?
- What constitutes an amendment quorum, notice period, public consultation and protected-design veto?

## FAQ addendum proposals (not yet assigned canonical Q numbers)
- Can everyone use KDN free? Core public access is intended to be free; actual service and funding model still under design.
- Is 2x profit repayment guaranteed? No. It is an unapproved proposal requiring legal review, and profit may be zero.
- Does Dasvandh buy governance rights? No. Optional recognition cannot override equal dignity or fundamental rights.
- Can any AI edit the constitution? AI may draft suggestions, but only authorized community review can adopt amendments.
- Are all 30 accounts connected? No; authorization and API availability must be checked one by one.
- Will mobile notifications appear automatically? Not yet; user permission and secure delivery infrastructure are required.
